ERRO
----
FOR UPDATE cannot be applied to the nullable side of an outer join

CAUSA CONFIRMADA
----------------
A consulta de StockBatchItem possuía `include: Material` e
`lock: transaction.LOCK.UPDATE` no mesmo SELECT.

O Sequelize gerava LEFT OUTER JOIN e o PostgreSQL recusava o FOR UPDATE.
